NeuroAI as an emerging anchor

Two complementary halves. Wei's lab uses network models and AI models to analyze neural data and to explain it. Pestilli's work builds deep-learning models and AI agents that analyze brain data. One half asks what AI can teach us about brains, the other what AI can do for brain science.

Why the data layer anchors it. NeuroAI scores 12 in cross-lab overlap, mid-table, because only two labs work in it deeply. It sits next to the two largest modes, simulation and mechanistic models (30) and neural data methods (28), and the dashed link on the map ties it to the data layer. Without shared data and compute, NeuroAI is two labs. With them, it is the scientific mission for the whole group.

The trend is visible elsewhere. Princeton lists NeuroAI as its own research area, Harvard's Kempner Institute has "AI and the Brain" as one of three areas, and UW's center lists NeuroAI 2026 as an upcoming event. UT could claim the data-and-compute version of it.

How this was built, and what to check

Source. OpenAlex author records and the 25 most-cited works per person, plus each person's topic counts and pairwise co-authorship searches. I coded each work's title and keywords into the themes and scored involvement from 0 (absent) to 3 (central).

NeuroAI scores. Scores for the NeuroAI theme, and Pestilli's AI-agent work, come from Pestilli's own description of the two labs, since agent work does not show up in the publication records used elsewhere.

Limits. Top-cited works skew toward older papers, so recent shifts in each lab are underweighted. All 21 pairs were searched for co-authored records. Only Seidemann–Priebe, Seidemann–Taillefumier (one paper, also with Priebe), Priebe–Wei and Priebe–Taillefumier returned any. Several profiles contained misattributed records (for example drug discovery, traffic and astronomy papers), which I excluded, and Taillefumier's work is split across several author records, so I used the main one. I read "Mike Mauck" as Michael Mauk. Pestilli's themes were scored from the same top-25 method as everyone else. Each person should confirm their own row.
